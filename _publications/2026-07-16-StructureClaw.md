StructureClaw explores traceable LLM agents for structural engineering workflows and introduces an executable benchmark for their evaluation. The open-source workspace connects natural-language engineering requests with modeling, validation, analysis, checks, and report generation. Its workflow records make intermediate artifacts and execution results available for inspection.
